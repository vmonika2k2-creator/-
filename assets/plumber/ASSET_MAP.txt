Plumber screen assets
=====================

The `figma` folder contains the locally downloaded image and SVG files used by
the Plumber service screen. Filenames are the original Figma asset UUIDs. They
are served by Flask from `/assets/plumber/figma/<filename>` so page rendering
does not depend on temporary Figma download links.

Primary visuals used by the screen:
- 90fc69de-b7c8-44f7-aa9c-0b3dca563ad6.png — plumber hero photo
- 7296da62-41a7-4bea-baa6-152593c03b14.png — VIP professional photo
- 310cb043-b56f-4adb-84ad-47d690bbb0ff.png — VIP card background
- 667316f1-4043-44f6-b8c5-c39152496a35.png — hero decorative background
- Category banners and service thumbnails are referenced in `plumber_data.py`.

The image content is from the supplied NoBroker Plumber Figma design. SVG files
are preserved alongside the PNG service photography and illustrations.
